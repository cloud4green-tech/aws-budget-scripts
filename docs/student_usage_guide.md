# Student Usage Guide

You can use AWS services freely until your assigned lab budget is close to the limit.

Budget: $2

- At $1.60 used, you receive a warning.
- At $1.90 used, high-cost services may be stopped or paused.
- At $2.00 used, the account becomes read-only.

No work is deleted by the platform. Stopped services can be reviewed by your trainer/admin.

For Bedrock, use the LMS/API Gateway proxy when provided. It gives friendly messages and avoids raw AWS permission errors.

Support: labs@cloud4green.com
